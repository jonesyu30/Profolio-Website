Finished on 22 Nov 2026.

This is crazy, officially done, the longest series of books i have ever read in my life. The author just said, what if the world collapses, and then this book is written. The entire universe, the physical laws, the humanity, human, love, responsibilities, everything is gone. There’s a feeling of helplessness, i am not sure if this is hindering my emotions these days, but there is definitely a feeling of suffocation at the end of the chapters, that human has already lost, as a species, it is sad, but it is just the law of the universe, everything dies, death is the only thing that goes to eternity. 
I dont know if this is a good ending, but it is an ending, like there isnt really anything after this, the universe will collapse. 

The memorable part at the end is that, like interstella, she missed her loved one in light speed, by 18 hundred thousand years, or something like that. She flew in light speed for 2 weeks, and everything is gone, everything about human and the three body, every society that could have existed is gone, in just 15 days. That is crazy, I was so anticipating the time where the 2 love ones meet each other after 4 centuries, but no, they just never see each other. That is how tough the world can be, how 冷血, how unforgiving, how little it cared. 

Welp, i would suppose my world is just the same, the law doesnt really contradict with modern science, tho normally, i wouldnt survive that long to witness anything, i probably wouldnt care, but the thought of missing all that potentially, or just the possibility that this might happen makes everything so uncertain. 

Just imagine being the person that is responsible for erasing humanity, twice, while being the last one who get to survive, how lonely, how guilty would it feel, surviving is the punishment to the acts. Welp, the story ended, the world has so many stories, so many little things, the series is huge, there should be like spin offs, fanfics, but i dont think i would get into those, i like this as it is, i liked the first book, where we know nothing about this world, just like how he warned, that we might not want to know the rules about the world, yet curiosity always wins. 

Lets take a look at the quotes :D

>如果她能早些知道，這個深愛他的男人一直跨越光年的距離守候在自己的身邊，那該是怎樣的安慰。

The guy that she killed. She asked him to die, yet he gifted her a planet. That is crazy right, and he keep loving her even after resurrected from the dead. 

>死亡是唯一一座永遠亮著的燈塔，不管你向哪裡航行，最終都得轉向它指引的方向。一切都會逝去，只有死神永生。

This is a foreshadow, to many chapters later. 

>像一副撲克牌，僅僅重洗而已。。。。。。可生命是一手同花順，一洗就什麼都沒了。

Crazy analogy right? Part of this book is just keep reminding us how little our lives are, being blown by the winds, washed away by the river, going with the flow of universe, and being lucky, or just existing because there are too much time. 

>但對於那些即將毀滅的更大的東西，她竭力避免自己去想，毀滅像一對黑色的巨翼遮蓋了她思想的天空，她不敢抬頭正視它

Sometimes, i just feel the same, not the part that would get destroyed, but the part where a dark shadow just cover my thoughts, that i am too scared to face it. 

>但最後發現，真正的力量在時間手裡，留下腳印比創造世界更難

>這場在太陽系邊緣的冷暗世界中的雪，使她感到了一絲母星的溫暖

How ironic, that the snowfall is the most warmth that she can feel, when everything about earth and the solar system is gone, gone in this dimension, cannot be observed. Snow is the only thing that feels familiar after this. 

> 她兩次處於僅次於上帝的位置上，卻兩次以愛的名義把世界推向深淵

Love, she did the right option in the sense of love, of humanity, of what we were taught to be the right thing, yet it might just have killed the species, two civilisation. 

> 在她過去的生活中，幸運的時光只是大災難和大毀滅之間的間隙，她對幸運有些恐懼了

She is the saddest character here, compare it to the first girl, the one who got his father killed brutally (I still remember that from the start of the first book, as it is just so vividly described, and is based on real history)

> 我的一生，就是在攀登一道責任的階梯

誰不是呢～Sad truth, i mean someone seems like they just dont care, i envy them, i look up to them, just living without responsibilities, they are enjoying life, doing the most important responsibility for using Earth resources, i mean why waste energy if you arent even happy right? Hedonism, this will be a topic for another time. 

Welp these quotes are all little sentences that just cannot describe the things that happened in this story, you need to know what happened in order to understand these quotes fully, so here i am again, asking you to read the whole book. It gets a bit boring at the third quarter, but then it is just the peace before the last disaster. This books is worth it, i think, i dont know, only if you have free time, or you are interested. But who wont be interested in the ending of the universe right? To read it is the only way to experience it, not by any summerizing videos online, sadly, half an hour of passive listening, just doesnt beat hours of active imagining and living. 

What a journey~
