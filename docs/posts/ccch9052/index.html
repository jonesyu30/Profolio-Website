    This lecture is seperated into two parts: explaining the course, and explaining Hendonism.

First example, The Truman Show, is a show about a guy living his dream life, yet it was actually just a scenery created for a show. His entire life is a lie. This brings the point that a life can be good subjectivly or objectively. Might Truman's life be bad objectively, but good subjectively?

    Another exmaple was 莊子's debate on wheather a fish is happy. 莊子 argues that we would not know what other is thinking if we aren't them. If we are not the fish, how can we possibly know what it is thinking.
